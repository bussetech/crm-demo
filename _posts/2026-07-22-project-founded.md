crm-demo is now part of [Bussetech Software Studio](https://bussetech.com):
CRM Demo — a deliberately generic multi-tenant CRM (organizations, people, activities, deals) run as a live public demonstration of the studio's SaaS capability; synthetic data only, published demo logins.

This post is the site's first feed item — the studio portal aggregates
`/feed.json` from every project site, so publishing a post here is how the
project surfaces on the portal's "latest across the studio" stream.
